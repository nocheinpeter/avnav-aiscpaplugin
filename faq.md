## How exact are the drawings?

Drawings may defer from realtiy due to 
- wrong AIS data sent
- wrong calculations
- bugs


<a name="real-ship-size-not-shown"></a>
## Real ship size not shown

- icon AISCPA is not checked
- real size display is disabled at the plugin settings
- ship dimension are not sent by AIS target
- GPS antenna position is not sent by AIS target or unkown
- needs minimum AvNav version 20261106
- zoom is less than configured min zoom in plugin settings
